OUR RESEARCH

Research Activities

SSRT conducts student-focused research and educational activities related to space science, astronomy and science education.

🌌 From Curiosity to Space Research

A student outreach and research initiative exploring interest in Space Science among students and encouraging greater awareness of space science and research opportunities.


The project uses questionnaires and educational interaction with students to understand their existing awareness and interest in Space Science.

RESEARCH PROCESS

How We Conduct Research

01 — Question

Identify a scientific or educational question worth investigating.

02 — Collect

Collect information through questionnaires, observations or other appropriate methods.

03 — Analyze

Organize and examine the information collected from the research activity.

04 — Share

Present findings and share scientific knowledge with students and the wider community.

STUDENT OUTREACH

Science Awareness & Outreach

SSRT also conducts student outreach activities designed to introduce learners to Space Science, astronomy and scientific research.


These activities can include questionnaires, discussions and educational sessions with students.

OUR APPROACH

Student Research

We believe research begins with curiosity. Students can start by asking questions, learning how to collect evidence and developing their own scientific thinking.
