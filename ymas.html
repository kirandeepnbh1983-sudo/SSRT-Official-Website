ABOUT YMAS

Learn About Space

Young Minds in Astronomy (YMAS) is a student learning initiative by Space Singh Researcher Team (SSRT).


The program encourages students to explore astronomy, space science and scientific thinking through structured learning experiences.

YMAS × SSRT APP

Learning Continues in the App

The SSRT App is the main platform for detailed YMAS learning content, courses, lessons, quizzes and course completion activities.


The website provides an overview of YMAS, while the SSRT App provides the complete learning experience.

LEARNING EXPERIENCE

Learn → Quiz → Complete

01 — Learn

02 — Quiz

Test your understanding through quizzes and learning activities.

03 — Complete

Complete the required activities for the selected learning program.

04 — Certificate

Eligible participants may receive a course completion certificate according to the program rules.
