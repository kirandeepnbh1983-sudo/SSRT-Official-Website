WHO WE ARE

About SSRT

Space Singh Researcher Team (SSRT) is a student-led initiative created to encourage students to explore space science, astronomy, scientific research, robotics and technology.


SSRT brings together students interested in learning, researching, building projects and sharing scientific knowledge with other students.

OUR MISSION

Research. Learn. Build.

Our mission is to create opportunities for students to learn about science and space, develop research skills and turn ideas into practical projects.

OUR AREAS

What SSRT Works On

🌌 Space Science

Astronomy, space exploration and learning about the universe.

🔬 Research

Student research activities, surveys and scientific investigations.

🤖 Robotics

Building and experimenting with robotics and technology.

📚 Education

Courses, outreach activities and student-focused learning.

FOUNDER

Founded by Ekam Singh

SSRT was founded by Ekam Singh with the aim of creating a student-led platform where young learners can explore space science, research, technology and innovation.

VISION

From Curiosity to Research

SSRT aims to encourage students to move beyond simply learning scientific facts and begin asking questions, conducting research and building solutions.
