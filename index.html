WHO WE ARE

Space Singh Researcher Team

Space Singh Researcher Team (SSRT) is a student-led initiative focused on space science, astronomy, research, robotics, technology and science education.

SSRT aims to provide students with opportunities to learn, explore scientific ideas, participate in research activities and build technology projects.
